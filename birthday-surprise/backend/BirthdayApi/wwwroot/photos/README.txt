Drop your photos in this folder:

- main.jpg   -> the single "hero" photo shown on the lock-unlock polaroid and
                on the cake page (filename is configurable in appsettings.json
                under Birthday:MainPhoto if you want to use a different name).
- anything else (e.g. photo1.jpg, memory2.png, us3.webp) -> automatically
  picked up and shown in the letter-page photo gallery. No code changes,
  no rebuild needed - just refresh the browser.

Supported formats: .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .gif
